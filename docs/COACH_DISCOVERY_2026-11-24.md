# Integrated Trade Coach discovery
One Coach, no separate trade finder. Existing guided goals, evaluation, workbench and negotiation remain. Discover screens all other league teams using current needs/lineup and existing teamMode, at most three candidate assets per team, then reuses coachOffers and the existing grade/receiver model. Both teams must have a practical roster benefit and a Toss-up-or-better result, fair market pricing, simulated receiving-manager willingness and no required roster cuts. Top results diversify partners before repeating. Empty results mean no candidate passed the bounded search, not that no trade exists. No valuation or negotiation weights changed. Current archetypes remain authoritative; the new research-only intelligence v0 is not yet validated and is not substituted. Existing private data remains private. Team Analyzer links to the same Coach; other-team views retain existing parity. Dashboard proactive feed, historic research, broad package generation and empirical acceptance calibration are future work.
