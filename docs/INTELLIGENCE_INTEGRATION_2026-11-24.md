- Manager profiles show recent and prior 180-day completed trade counts, known package shapes, acquisitions and trade-day pick-capital directions. - Discover expands to five single targets per partner and at most one two-target package. Existing bilateral practical benefit, price, cut and negotiation gates remain. Bounded, not exhaustive.
- Today surfaces roster action ideas and direct Discover link; does not generate untested offers or run expensive analysis on every page load.
- Trade Lab explains exclusions after the next successful market repricing. Sealed collector emits aggregate queue receipt after successful encrypted publication; no raw league IDs or snapshots. Queue coverage is not historical data completeness.
- Weekly six-format public archive benchmark: deterministic sampled player-only one-for-two deals, additive vs consolidation sensitivity against later additive KTC values, 2024–25 retrospective, not sealed holdout. Results are validated and uploaded as separate CI artifacts, not deployed as valuation changes. No new Sleeper calls. 
- Remaining evidence gates: first production queue/coverage publish, actual benchmark runs, independent league/time holdouts, private research-computer decryption and complete pre-trade snapshots, opt-in rejected offers, future player/rookie/roster model calibration, other external pricing baselines, browser acceptance and profiling. No claim of historical forecast accuracy or market superiority before those results.
