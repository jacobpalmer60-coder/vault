# Consolidation disagreement
Do not treat KTC package adjustment as observed market premium or force a desired winner on one completed trade. The live calculator now compares KTC-adjusted direction and gap directly to Vault market pricing, not only the retired Vault package curve. The warning is visible by default whenever their gaps differ by at least 15 percentage points or materially reverse direction; the underlying offers, roster grades and market weights are unchanged. Historical market estimate uses dated evidence in PR 36. Further consolidation calibration must include mixed player/pick packages, independent future outcomes and actual pre-trade snapshots; a completed transaction alone cannot establish fair price or future value.
