# Recommendation quality: phase 1
## Evidence boundaries
The existing separated-price benchmark compares absolute future additive market value. The larger package can win that comparison even if its growth is worse. Keep additive prices as the live market-price measure; do not interpret a higher agreement rate as investment skill or acceptance.
## New tests
- Relative growth: player-only completed packages, same-format trade-day prices, previous 90-day trend and 365-day observed prices. Seven-calendar-day strictly backward maximum lookup. Separate absolute future gap and relative appreciation outcomes. No pick proxies; unknown prices and duplicate or ambiguous identities excluded. Known future zero retained.
- 2024 training with labels mature before 2025-08-01, 2025 test origins from that date, hashed league-season keys disjoint. One fixed regularized, orientation-symmetric trend coefficient; compare identical rows to unchanged price. All six formats in scheduled/manual CI; new main push also triggers first run. Reports only, no automatic live tuning.
- Live Coach audit: real browser, every roster, warmed manager evidence; measure search completion, offers, partner coverage, asset ownership, duplicate packages, both-side evidence, roster limits, additive gap, grades, shape diversity and latency. Empty list is descriptive, not failure by itself. Local anonymous roster IDs; no trades submitted. Run from repo with installed Playwright: node scripts/audit-coach-quality.cjs LEAGUE_ID.
## What is not established
Relative growth is not production, playoff probability or roster fit; old archive may overlap existing calibration and league-season hashes cannot prove manager independence. Same players and price provider may occur in both partitions. Further point-in-time roster context and independently sourced values or prospective outcomes are needed. Completed trades cannot identify manager acceptance probability. No superiority claim or live weight change.
