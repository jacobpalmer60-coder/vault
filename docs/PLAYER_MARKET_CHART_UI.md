# Player profiles, market and chart experience
Keep existing prices, sources, watchlist storage and projections. Profile displays value, positional rank, PPG, market estimate and price history first; expand for VORP, weekly/season performance and completed trades. Defer the existing 9 MB projected-stat history request until performance details are opened; update the PPG chart in place rather than resetting the dialog. Add native keyboard and tap chart inspection, distinct tooltip ID, bounded chart registry. League chart legends become native buttons with persistent single-team highlighting and reset; all team series and trade marks remain. Market lookup shows watched players from the existing local watchlist, not a new account or service. Method caveats remain available. This release requires browser and real-league checks; no valuation changes or additional Sleeper endpoints.
