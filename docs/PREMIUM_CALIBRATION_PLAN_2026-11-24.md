# Independent player and pick premium calibration
Initial SF report (local, sample 1/10): 1,088 player-only completed prices and 4,930 pick-including packages; full premium does not minimize mean absolute gap in the overall cohorts. Trend growth forecast loses to unchanged prices on 991 complete cases. Do not deploy trend.
Phase 2: fit 25 fixed player/pick premium pairs on older 2024 rows only, with minimum 200 trades, 100 mixed packages, 30 league-season keys. Evaluate selected pair on later 2025 disjoint hashed league keys using identical rows for raw, full, and candidate. Print price disagreement, paired differences and exploratory league-cluster intervals across package types and months. Sparse cohorts stay visible. No automatic weight update. No test-set tuning.
Future decision: confirm across formats and prospectively timestamp premiums before collecting new transactions, validate price agreement independently of recommendation and future roster utility, then consider a bounded shadow rollout. Historical snapshots have incomplete provenance and cannot demonstrate prospective performance. Pick data is mid-round proxy, not slot. No acceptance claims without offer/rejection observations.
