What the numbers mean

Show ▾Hide ▴
Overall — Players + Picks combined, a team's total dynasty value at KeepTradeCut prices
QB, RB, WR, TE, Picks — The team's rank in the league at each position by the value of its players there (and its picks), 1 = best. Green is among the strongest, yellow the middle of the league, red a need
Picks — Total KTC value of every future draft pick this team owns, R1-4 live from Sleeper
PPG — Points actually scored per game so far (Sleeper's points for), and below it the projection: points per game if you started the best possible lineup from your full roster the rest of the season, on Sleeper's projections in your league's scoring. The projection updates every few hours as rosters, injuries and projections change; playoff and title odds blend both, leaning more on actual points as the season goes
VORP (Value Over Replacement Player) — Same lineup as Lineup PPG, but each starter's points are measured against "replacement level" at their position (the best player still sitting on waivers) instead of from zero. Raw PPG always favors QBs since they score more at every tier, even on waivers — VORP strips that out, so a team's hard-to-replace edge is comparable across QB/RB/WR/TE instead of just rewarding whoever plays the highest-scoring position. Per-player breakdown on Player Rankings.
Longevity — How many of the next 5 years this roster is projected to stay good enough for the playoffs, as players age and picks arrive. Not age or pick count — a young, pick-poor roster that's already elite can out-project a pick-rich one that hasn't hit yet.
Archetype — Build type, from how you stand now vs. how long that lasts (see guide below)
Career — All-time W-L-T for this manager, across every past season of this league on Sleeper
Proj. Record / Playoff % / Title % — The rest of your schedule played out thousands of times, with each team scoring around its Lineup PPG each week (give or take 22%). A rough estimate, not a guarantee

Archetypes

Every archetype comes from two independent standings — Contending (current player value + production: how strong you are right now) and Longevity (the 5-year projection: how many years that lasts) — each ranked Top 2 / Top Half / Bottom Half / Bottom 2 of the league. Draft capital (Picks) is tracked separately — shown as its own qualifier next to each pill — since a team's picks and its projected window don't always point the same direction.
